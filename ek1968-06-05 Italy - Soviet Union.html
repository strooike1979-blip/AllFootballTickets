Italy

Match Result

The match ended in a goalless draw after 120 minutes. With no penalty shootouts at the time, Italy advanced to the final via a coin toss.

Historical Note: Italian captain Giacinto Facchetti chose "heads" in the dressing room with the referee. Italy won the toss and the right to play in the final on home soil.

Match Info

Stadium: Stadio San Paolo, Naples

Attendance: 68,582

Referee: Kurt Tschenscher (West Germany)
